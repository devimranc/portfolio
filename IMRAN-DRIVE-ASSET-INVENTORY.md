## How folders were mapped

Each top-level folder stays in one category.

Character model is the stylized character set already published under 3D Modeling & Character Modeling. It includes the stills and the rig-with-expressions clip, so the folder stays there.

AI character animation, AI Environment, AI product animation, and Rita AI scenes are under AI-Assisted Video & Animation. AI Environment is the rain clip already on the Work page. The two Parasite script documents sit directly in `imran-drive/` and match the Rita AI Parasite scenes, so they are listed in that category rather than inside a folder.

2D Game Art is the dragon game-art set already published under 2D Animation & Motion Design. Four dragon frames with the same filenames also sit in `imran-drive/` itself, beside AllSocials.WEBP. Motion graphics is the motion-design folder.

Mson PC components is two product stills, under Product Modeling & Visualization. 3D Brand Intro, the DRUO About Us voiceover, and Puffer jacket are loose clips under Product Animation & Exploded Views. Scene, monologue, snow, comedy, and burn-down clips are loose files under Character Rigging & Animation.

No folder in this drive is an Unreal, VR, or architectural visualization set, so those categories are empty. Files whose names do not identify a discipline are unmapped: numbered or hash-named media, a copied movie, a thumbnail, and a job-description document.
